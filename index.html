The mean and median both describe the center, but they respond differently when a score changes.

Teaching note: Exactly 50 observations. The median is the average of the 25th and 26th sorted values. Moving a single observation by 10 points changes the mean by 10/50 = 0.2, provided its new value stays on the 0–100 scale. Values are illustrative, not actual participant data.
